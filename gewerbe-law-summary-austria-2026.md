# Austrian Gewerbe: Easy Legal and Cost Summary (2026)

## What this document covers

This is a plain-language summary of the four WKO pages provided. It explains the
main rules shown in those pages for an Austrian Gewerbe in 2026.

It is not individual legal or tax advice. The correct result depends on the
registered trade, other employment, income, age, and personal circumstances.
Confirm important decisions with WKO, SVS, Finanzamt, or a tax adviser.

## 1. SVS compulsory insurance

Someone who becomes self-employed in Austria is normally insured with the SVS
under the GSVG. The insurance usually starts automatically on the date of the
Gewerbeanmeldung, unless an exemption applies.

SVS contribution notices are issued quarterly, normally in February, May, August,
and November.

### Minimum contribution basis in 2026

For the first three calendar years, contributions are provisionally calculated
using the minimum basis of **EUR 551.10 per month**:

| Insurance | Rate | Monthly | Quarterly | Yearly |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pension insurance (PV) | 18.50% | EUR 101.95 | EUR 305.85 | EUR 1,223.40 |
| Health insurance (KV) | 6.80% | EUR 37.47 | EUR 112.41 | EUR 449.64 |
| Self-employed provision (SeVo) | 1.53% | EUR 8.43 | EUR 25.29 | EUR 101.16 |
| Accident insurance (UV) | Fixed | EUR 12.95 | EUR 38.85 | EUR 155.40 |
| **Total provisional payment** | **26.83% plus fixed UV** | **EUR 160.80** | **EUR 482.40** | **EUR 1,929.60** |

These are minimum planning figures from the supplied 2026 information. If the
final contribution basis is higher, SVS can later request additional pension and
health contributions. Keep money aside for this possible adjustment.

From the fourth calendar year, the provisional contribution is generally based
on income from the relevant earlier tax assessment rather than only the minimum
basis.

The first and second calendar years have a founding bonus: there is no later
health-insurance reassessment for those years according to the supplied WKO
information.

### If you are also employed

If you have simultaneous compulsory insurance through employment, the insured
employment income and self-employed income are coordinated. For 2026, the
maximum contribution basis shown is **EUR 97,020.00**. If the total would
otherwise fall below the minimum basis of **EUR 6,613.20 per year**, SVS adjusts
the assessment.

## 2. Possible SVS exemption for small businesses

An exemption from compulsory SVS insurance may be available for a small
Einzelunternehmen if all relevant conditions are met:

- Annual turnover from the business is below **EUR 55,000**.
- Annual profit from the business is below **EUR 6,613.20**.
- The person has not been insured under the GSVG for more than 12 calendar
  months in the previous five years.

Important:

- The exemption is possible only for an **Einzelunternehmen**, not every legal
  form.
- It is **not automatic**; an application is required.
- SVS checks the limits later using the tax assessment.
- The limits are not automatically indexed according to the supplied page.
- The exemption does **not** remove accident insurance.

Accident insurance remains payable at **EUR 12.95 per month**, **EUR 38.85 per
quarter**, or **EUR 155.40 per year**.

The supplied WKO page also mentions special rules for people who have completed
their 60th year, people who have completed their 57th year and met the limits
for the previous five years, and certain maternity/childcare situations.

The page warns that rules connected with receiving AMS benefits changed on
1 January 2026. Anyone receiving AMS support should ask the AMS adviser before
starting an additional activity.

## 3. Income tax

Income tax is based on total taxable income. This includes:

- Profit from the Gewerbe.
- Income from employment.
- Other taxable income.

The business profit is broadly:

```text
Business revenue - legitimate business expenses = business profit
```

The 2026 income-tax bands shown in the supplied document are:

| Taxable annual income | Marginal rate |
|---:|---:|
| Up to EUR 13,539 | 0% |
| EUR 13,539 to EUR 21,992 | 20% |
| EUR 21,992 to EUR 36,458 | 30% |
| EUR 36,458 to EUR 70,365 | 40% |
| EUR 70,365 to EUR 104,859 | 48% |
| EUR 104,859 to EUR 1,000,000 | 50% |
| Above EUR 1,000,000 | 55% |

These rates are progressive. Reaching a higher band does not mean that all
income is taxed at that higher rate.

Without a tax representative, the income-tax return is normally due by the end
of June of the following year, according to the supplied WKO page.

## 4. VAT (Umsatzsteuer)

### Small-business exemption

If annual turnover is no more than **EUR 55,000 gross**, and the previous year's
turnover also meets the condition, the small-business VAT exemption generally
applies automatically.

Under this exemption:

- You do not add VAT to your invoices.
- You do not pay collected VAT to Finanzamt.
- You normally cannot reclaim input VAT on business purchases.

Do not show VAT on an invoice by mistake. If VAT is shown, the invoiced VAT can
become payable to Finanzamt.

### Exceeding the limit

The supplied document describes a tolerance of up to 10%, meaning up to
**EUR 60,500 gross**. The exact VAT consequences depend on how and when the
limit is exceeded. VAT registration and invoicing may become necessary.

Turnover can include other taxable turnover, such as rental income. Certain
VAT-exempt turnover, such as some medical services, is treated differently.

### Voluntary VAT registration

Even below EUR 55,000, a business can opt into VAT by submitting the required
declaration to Finanzamt. According to the supplied information, this choice
normally binds the business for at least five years.

Voluntary VAT can be useful when:

- Customers are mainly VAT-registered businesses.
- The business has significant VAT-bearing expenses.
- Reclaiming input VAT is more valuable than the simpler exemption.

## 5. Ancillary services and trade limits

As a trade operator, you may offer services covered by your registered trade.
You may also offer certain services from other trades as ancillary services
(*Nebenrechte*), if they economically and sensibly complement your main trade.

The supplied WKO information gives these limits:

- Ancillary services from free trades: no more than **30% of annual revenue**.
- Ancillary services from regulated trades: no more than **15% of the value of
  the relevant order**.

These services must genuinely complement the main business. They are not a way
to operate a separate trade without the required registration or qualification.
Exceeding the limits may result in unauthorized trade operation, administrative
penalties, and civil-law consequences.

## 6. Practical meaning for a small SaaS Gewerbe

For a small SaaS business, the main items to plan for are:

1. Register the correct trade description.
2. Expect SVS payments unless an exemption is approved.
3. Keep a separate record of revenue, expenses, and profit.
4. Reserve money for income tax and possible later SVS adjustments.
5. Decide whether the small-business VAT exemption is suitable.
6. Check VAT rules carefully for EU and non-EU customers.
7. Keep invoices and receipts.
8. Ask WKO whether planned software, consulting, marketing, or other services
   fit within the registered trade and its ancillary rights.

## Important next checks

Before registering or applying for an exemption, confirm:

- The exact Gewerbe and whether it is free or regulated.
- Whether the business will be an Einzelunternehmen.
- Whether you are employed at the same time.
- Whether you receive AMS benefits.
- Whether your expected turnover and profit meet the SVS exemption limits.
- Whether your customers are in Austria, other EU countries, or outside the EU.
- Whether VAT exemption or voluntary VAT registration is financially better.
