# Godwit — Pilot Program Agreement

*A one-page summary for pilot venues. This sits alongside, and does not replace, the full [Terms of Service](/terms) and [Privacy Notice](/privacy).*

## What this is

You're one of a small number of venues trying Godwit during its first local pilot. The goal is real feedback on the product, not a long-term contract. Either of us can end the pilot at any time by saying so.

## Your data stays yours

This is the part we want to be completely clear about:

- **You own the feedback and contact data your customers (voters) give you** through your polls - their opted-in emails, prize-draw entries, and any private messages they send you.
- **Godwit only stores and processes that data on your behalf**, to run the service for you. We never use it for our own marketing, and we never sell it to anyone.
- Your workspace is the data controller for that data under GDPR; Godwit is your data processor. See "Data controller vs. processor" in the [Terms of Service](/terms) for what this means in practice.
- If you close your workspace or ask us to delete your data, we will, subject to the retention rules described in the Privacy Notice.

## What you're responsible for

- Making sure your polls, questions, and any prize draws or review requests comply with the law in your own location (Godwit builds in the required disclosures, but you're the one running the promotion).
- Keeping your login and any staff invitations secure.
- Using the product for genuine customer feedback, not for the restricted content categories described in the Terms of Service.

## What we're responsible for

- Keeping your workspace's data isolated from every other venue's.
- Being reachable for support, bugs, and questions during the pilot.
- Telling you directly if something changes that affects your data or these terms.
- Because this is an early pilot, we don't yet promise a specific uptime or support-response time - if that's a blocker for you, tell us and we'll figure out what's realistic together.
