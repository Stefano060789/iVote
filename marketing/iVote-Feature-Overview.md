# iVote — Full Feature Overview
### QR-Based Feedback & Engagement Platform for Physical Venues
*Prepared for marketing use — September 2026*

---

## 1. What iVote Is

iVote turns any physical location — a restaurant table, a gym, a salon chair, a hotel room, an event entrance, a hospital waiting room — into a measurable feedback channel. A guest scans a single QR code, taps one answer, and the venue instantly sees what's working and what isn't. No app download, no account, no friction for the guest.

**Core promise:** *Turn every physical location into a measurable feedback channel.*

## 5. Feedback Intelligence & Recovery

| Feature | Marketing Angle |
|---|---|
| **Automatic alert rules** | Get notified the moment a low score or a specific negative answer comes in — catch problems in real time, not in a monthly report |
| **Vote-volume anomaly detection** | The system watches for sudden drops in feedback volume and flags them automatically — an early warning system, not just a dashboard |

| **Feedback recovery workflow** | Turn any alert into an assigned recovery task and track it through to resolution |
| **Weekly email reports** | An automatic Monday summary of poll activity, alerts, and recovery status, delivered straight to the manager's inbox |
| **"We heard you" public updates** | Venues can publicly post "We fixed the Wi-Fi you complained about" — visibly closing the feedback loop builds real guest trust |
| **Private voter messages** | Guests can send a direct private message to the venue while voting — a built-in, frictionless complaint channel that never has to go to a public review |

## 9. What Makes iVote Different

1. **It's a two-way loop, not just a survey tool.** Rewards, prize draws, review routing, and public "we heard you" updates all close the loop back to the guest — most QR feedback tools stop at data collection.
2. **It's built for physical locations first.** Reusable QR codes, rotating polls, bulk QR generation, and location-level analytics are designed around real printed materials and real venues, not just online forms.
3. **It gets smarter over time.** Anomaly detection, AI sentiment tagging, and benchmark reports turn raw feedback into proactive insight instead of a spreadsheet someone has to read manually.
4. **It's already enterprise-shaped.** Multi-tenant security, team roles, an API, webhooks, and compliance controls are in place from day one — the platform can grow from a single café to a multi-location chain without being rebuilt.
